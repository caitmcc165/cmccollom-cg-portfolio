Supply Mover

Introducing a business system that could support warehouse and commercial technician workflows.

TAG Chemical TechnologiesOperations Manager

The need

Spreadsheet-based tracking made it harder to keep client, inventory, product, and delivery information organized across a busy operation.

My contribution

    02 / Professional implementation · 2017

    Schedulicity

    Making the scheduling experience more organized for staff and customers—and imagining what comes next.

    White Dragon Martial ArtsOperations Manager & Lead Instructor

    The need

    Scheduling and customer coordination were core daily workflows for a martial arts school with classes, instructors, and ongoing student programs.

    My contribution

    Selected, introduced, and trained users on Schedulicity while coordinating scheduling practices alongside membership and billing processes.

    The outcome

    A shared scheduling workflow and more consistent day-to-day coordination. Across my tenure, I also helped grow enrollment from 86 to 172 students through sales and retention efforts.
