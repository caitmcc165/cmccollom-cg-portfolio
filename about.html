My work has always been about people, teaching, systems, and finding better ways forward. Technology is a natural extension of that.

Hi again, I'm Caitlin

From running a business to improving its tools.

Across business operations and client education, I've coordinated people, solved problems, supported training, and taken ownership of the details that keep work moving.

What I enjoyed most wasn't simply managing a schedule or overseeing a team. It was figuring out where a process was breaking down, finding a better system, and helping other people feel comfortable using it.

At Technical Difference HRIS (People-Trak), I coordinated client training and prepared clear instructional materials. At White Dragon Martial Arts, I introduced scheduling software, developed instructors, and helped grow the school. At TAG Chemical Technologies, I helped implement business software and documented warehouse and client workflows.

I graduated from Western Governors University in 2026 with an M.S. in DevOps, building technical knowledge alongside years of practical operations and client education experience. My goal is to bring that grounded perspective to software implementation, customer enablement, and technical coordination—without losing sight of the humans on the other side of the screen.
