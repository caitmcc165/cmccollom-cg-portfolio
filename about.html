Hi again, I'm Caitlin

From running a business to improving its tools.

For more than a decade, I worked in small-business operations—coordinating people, solving problems, supporting customers, and taking ownership of the details that kept the day running.

What I enjoyed most wasn't simply managing a schedule or overseeing a team. It was figuring out where a process was breaking down, finding a better system, and helping other people feel comfortable using it.

At TAG Chemical Technologies, that included software implementation and documentation for warehouse and client workflows. At White Dragon Martial Arts, it meant adopting scheduling software, developing instructors, supporting student families, and growing the school.

Now I'm building on those experiences through graduate study in DevOps. My goal is to bring a grounded business perspective to software implementation, customer enablement, and technical coordination—without losing sight of the humans on the other side of the screen.
